About

Get ready to dive into the future of electronic music as DGTL Amsterdam returns to the NDSM Docklands on March 26–28, 2027 in Amsterdam.

Experience cutting-edge stages, forward-thinking sounds, and a weekend full of art, innovation, and collective energy. From globally renowned names to tomorrow's trailblazers, we bring together a diverse lineup that defines the forefront of electronic culture.

Join us this spring for an unforgettable weekend of connection, creativity, and pure dance floor magic.
