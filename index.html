Our story

At DGTL, we constantly strive for a balance between the leading names in art and music, the newest discoveries and exciting local treasures.

We design impressive stages within raw, industrial settings. Partnering with the best VJs, we create mesmerising light effects and visuals. In addition to celebrating electronic music and art, DGTL is a space for expression and exploration of a revolutionary sustainability project.

Our latest DGTL x Vertere Berlin collection just dropped

What started as a collaboration has grown into a natural creative connection. Vertere Berlin is founded by a collective of DJs and designers, sharing the same philosophy: nightlife is more than music. It's a space where people come together, express themselves, and move freely.

Together, we've created a capsule that reflects that feeling. Unisex pieces inspired by movement, unity and the universal energy of the dance floor. Limited edition for a single night of festival.

DGTL Records

We combine both upcoming talents together with established artists, bringing unique music and cross-over sounds.

With DGTL Records we build on our festival's musical theme of an evocative cross section of sounds and styles: showcasing forward thinking, cutting edge artists. Besides this, the label also functions as a platform for newcomers. This results in the creation of a coherent selection of music with a true family vibe; we want artists to feel connected to the label, the people behind it and the other artists.

Our in-house label DGTL Records emerged from the DGTL festival brand and has a single focus on music and community.
